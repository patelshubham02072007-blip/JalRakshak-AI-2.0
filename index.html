Research Protocol & Evaluation Plan

01

Dataset preparation

Collect rainfall, water availability and demand records with units, dates, geographic level and source provenance.

02

Baseline comparison

Compare a consumption-only baseline with a rainfall- and demand-aware approach using a chronological holdout.

03

Model evaluation

Use MAE/RMSE for numeric forecasting and precision, recall and F1 for risk classification when verified labels exist.

Proposed research contribution

JalRakshak AI connects water-cover estimation, area-level comparison, conservation scenarios and explanations in one software workflow. This is a proposed integration to be assessed through literature review and experiments; global uniqueness or model accuracy is not claimed.

Research limitations

  • India-wide is the intended study scope; actual coverage depends on comparable datasets.
  • Reservoir storage, groundwater estimates and household tank storage are different measures and must not be casually combined.
  • Greywater only offsets suitable non-potable freshwater demand where reuse is safe and practical.
  • Flow/pressure anomaly screening is not confirmation of a leak without suitable time-series meter data.
  • No trained ML model, live government feed, live GIS map or official warning service is claimed in this build.
